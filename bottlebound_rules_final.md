# BOTTLEBOUND

**A Live-Action Fantasy Skirmish Game**

2 teams • 12 players • 1 referee • 30–60 minute match

*Core rules and final character abilities*

## 1. Game Overview

BOTTLEBOUND is a two-team outdoor skirmish game. Each player controls one character represented by a bottle. Characters move through a bounded wooded battlefield, use physical ball attacks and one-use abilities, and are Downed when their HP reaches 0. The first team to Down all six opposing characters wins, subject to the 60-minute final-round rule.

## 3. Equipment

- Character bottles: 12 empty, unbreakable bottles or cans, one per player. The bottle is the character’s exact battlefield position.

- Shared arsenal: A pool of soft throwable balls. A ball is always available when a legal physical attack is made. The referee or neutral ball handlers keep the active player supplied.

- Initiative: One d20 per player.

- Boundary markers: Natural landmarks, cones, rope, flags, or other safe markers may be used to make the playable area clear.

## 4. Battlefield Setup

- The referee establishes a finite playable area before initiative is rolled. A roughly 20 × 20 pace area is a useful guideline, but the exact shape and size should fit the terrain.

- The battlefield should be large enough for 12 players to spread out, but small enough that a Ranged character cannot retreat indefinitely.

- Natural terrain—trees, bushes, roots, slopes, and uneven ground—creates cover and tactical movement constraints.

- The referee assigns and shows all 12 starting positions. Players do not roll, draft, or choose them.

- Starting positions should be fair rather than perfectly symmetrical. Neither team should begin with an obvious advantage in cover, distance, elevation, or access.

- Players may move along the boundary but may never intentionally move their bottle outside it. If movement would cross the boundary, the unused movement may be redirected along a legal path.

- The referee may disallow unsafe movement, unsafe throwing positions, or unsafe terrain. Safety overrides game positioning.

## 5. Core Terms

**Character / Bottle:** The bottle is the character for all gameplay purposes. The player and bottle move together. Players themselves are never targets, cover, or Line-of-Sight blockers.

**Turn:** One character’s opportunity to act.

**Round:** A full pass through the fixed initiative order. Downed characters are skipped. A revived character returns to its original initiative position.

**Action:** One of two things a character may do on its turn. A Basic Attack, Standard Ability, Powerful Ability, or Move uses one action.

**Line of Sight: Bottle-to-bottle visibility, as judged by the referee. Partial natural cover may still allow Line of Sight; complete or insufficient visibility blocks it. There is no fixed percentage test.**

Throw: The physical hand-thrown ball event. A Throw is not automatically an Attack unless it is being used by a Basic Attack or an ability that can deal damage.

Attack: Any game effect capable of dealing damage. An ability with Attack Type: None is not an Attack and does not trigger Reactions that require an Attack.

Active: A character that is not Downed.

**Ally: Any character on the same team, including a Downed character.**

**Enemy: Any character on the opposing team, including a Downed character.**

**Downed:** A character at 0 HP. Its bottle is laid flat at its current position. It cannot act, move, or use abilities. Downed does not mean dead; BOTTLEBOUND does not track death as a game state.

## 6. Initiative & Game Clock

- Before the game begins, all 12 players roll a d20 and add their Initiative Modifier from the roster table.

- The referee records one master 1–12 initiative list from highest total to lowest. Exact ties are broken immediately by coin flip.

- The initiative order never changes. Downed characters are skipped. A revived character returns at its original initiative position: if that position has not yet occurred in the current round, it acts normally when reached; if it has already passed, it waits until the next round.

- After the battlefield, starting positions, and initiative order are ready, the referee announces the start and begins the 60-minute game clock.

- The referee may pause the clock for real-world safety or unavoidable external interruptions.

## 7. Turn Structure & Movement

**On each turn, a character has two actions. A Move, Basic Attack, Standard Ability, or Powerful Ability uses one action. Any two actions may be taken in either order.**

- Normal movement may be 0, 1, or 2 natural walking paces and may change direction between paces.

- The bottle moves with the player and marks the character’s new position immediately.

Normal movement must follow a safe legal path. A bottle may not finish outside the battlefield or overlapping another bottle.

- A Move action moves the bottle up to 2 paces. Range and Line of Sight are checked from the bottle’s position at the exact moment an action or ability is used.

- A character may take Move twice in one turn.

- A character may use any two valid actions, including two Basic Attacks, two Abilities, two Moves, or any combination of them.

- A Powerful Ability uses one action. It does not grant normal movement unless its card explicitly says so. Movement explicitly granted by the Powerful Ability itself is still allowed.

- A character may take zero, one, or two actions.

- Reactions happen outside the normal turn economy and do not consume an action.

## 8. Basic Attacks

- Every character has an unlimited Basic Attack. A Basic Attack is a physical hand throw using one ball and deals 1 damage to each legal bottle it physically hits.

- Melee Basic Attack: hard maximum range of 2 paces.

- Ranged Basic Attack: hard maximum range of 6 paces.

- Range is measured bottle-to-bottle from the attacker’s position. Every bottle affected by the attack must be within the attack’s legal range.

Every bottle affected by a physical attack must also have bottle-to-bottle Line of Sight from the attacker’s bottle at the moment the attack is made. Partial cover may still allow Line of Sight under the referee’s judgment.

- Physical throws do not use target declarations. The player simply throws. If the ball hits an ally, an enemy, the thrower’s own bottle, or multiple bottles, each legal bottle hit takes the attack’s damage and effects.

- A bottle may be affected at most once by a single physical attack, even if the ball contacts it more than once.

- The first contact with terrain—such as the ground, a tree, root, or other obstacle—ends the attack at that point. Bottles validly hit before that contact remain affected; bottles contacted afterward are not. There is no intentional ricochet rule.

Any physical contact between the attack ball and a bottle counts as a hit; the bottle does not need to topple or visibly move. If the ball leaves the battlefield, the attack ends immediately.

- If a damaging physical hit moves or knocks over a bottle, the referee resolves damage and then returns the bottle to its pre-attack position. Damage never causes movement unless an ability explicitly says so.

- Wind, accidental bumps, feet, uneven ground, or other non-gameplay movement deal 0 damage. The referee resets the bottle to its correct position.

## 9. Abilities

- Each character has 2 abilities. Each ability may be used once per game unless its card explicitly says otherwise.

- An ability is spent once it is legally activated, even if a later Reaction completely prevents its effect. An invalid declaration does not spend the ability.

- Only targeted abilities require a target declaration. Physical throws do not.

- An ability target is declared when the ability is activated. The referee confirms legality, including range and Line of Sight where required.

Targeted offensive abilities cannot target a Downed character unless the ability explicitly says they can.

- Physical ability attacks follow all normal physical-throw rules unless the card explicitly overrides them. Their effects are carried by the ball and apply to every legal bottle physically hit, including accidental allies or the attacker’s own bottle, provided that bottle is within the attack’s hard range and has Line of Sight from the original attacker’s bottle.

- Non-physical Ability Attacks affect only their declared target unless the card explicitly says otherwise.

- All temporary buffs and debuffs attached to a character end immediately when that affected character becomes Downed. The character who created the effect becoming Downed does not remove an already-applied effect.

If a temporary effect is written to expire on the source character’s next turn, it expires at the end of that source character’s next scheduled initiative position even if the source is Downed and therefore skipped.

- Revival does not restore previous temporary effects.

## 10. Reactions & Attack Resolution

**Physical attack resolution**

1\. The ball is thrown. As it contacts bottles, the referee determines which contacts are legal hits under range, Line of Sight, terrain, and once-per-attack rules.

2\. Before damage or effects from a legal hit are finalized, any character whose Reaction trigger is legally met may react. A Reaction may therefore protect an ally even when the reacting character was not hit.

3\. A projectile-redirection Reaction interrupts the ball at the moment of contact. The redirected ball then continues as the same attack, with the same source, hard range, and attached ball effects; later legal hits are resolved normally.

4\. For each affected character, calculate all applicable damage increases and attached effects, then apply legal Reactions, reductions, and prevention.

5\. Finalize damage and effects separately for each affected character.

6\. Apply HP loss. A character that reaches 0 HP is immediately Downed.

7\. Immediately check for team elimination.

**Targeted Ability Attack resolution**

1\. Declare and legally activate the ability and its target.

2\. Allow legal Reactions.

3\. Calculate increases, reductions, and prevention.

4\. Finalize damage and effects.

5\. Apply HP loss. A character that reaches 0 HP is immediately Downed.

6\. Immediately check for team elimination.

- One character may use at most one Reaction against a single triggering attack. Different characters may react to the same attack whenever their own Reaction triggers are independently met.

- A Reaction that protects one character does not erase the attack for other characters unless the card explicitly says so.

- If multiple legal Reactions are declared, they are declared before damage is finalized and resolved consistently by the referee.

- Partial damage reduction is legal. Final damage cannot be below 0.

- Overkill damage has no additional effect. HP cannot fall below 0.

## 11. Damage, Combos & Persistent Effects

- Multiple legal ability effects may apply to the same attack. Effects from different abilities stack unless a card explicitly says otherwise.

- There is no universal cap on total damage from a combined attack. The intended balance is that players can set up powerful team combos.

- An individual ability may contribute no more than its written damage. The current ruleset does not use damage-over-time effects.

- Character-based effects such as Hunter’s Mark or Hex attach to the affected character and trigger when their written condition is met.

- Ball-based effects such as Backstab travel with that physical ball for the duration of that attack.

- If an attack is reduced to 0 finalized damage, it does not count as a successful damaging hit for effects that require damage to trigger.

- Damage is always resolved immediately. No poison, burning, bleeding, or other periodic damage is used.

## 12. HP, Healing, Downed State & Revival

- Characters use the maximum HP shown in the roster table: 3, 4, or 5 HP.

- Healing restores exactly 1 HP unless an ability explicitly says otherwise and can never exceed the target’s current maximum HP.

- Ordinary healing cannot affect a Downed character. Only abilities that explicitly revive may do so.

- At 0 HP, a character is immediately Downed. Lay its bottle flat exactly where it was. The character cannot move, act, or use abilities.

- A Downed bottle remains at that location and does not gain tactical movement from incidental contact.

- A revived character returns at 1 HP, stands upright in the same location, and is immediately vulnerable again. It returns to its original initiative position: if that position is still ahead in the current round, it acts normally when reached; if that position has already passed, it waits until the next round. Revival never grants an immediate extra turn.

- Revival does not restore spent abilities or previous temporary buffs/debuffs.

- If all six characters on a team are Downed at the same time, that team is immediately and permanently eliminated. A later revival cannot restore an eliminated team.

## 13. Victory & the 60-Minute Limit

- A team wins immediately when all six characters on the opposing team are Downed at the same time.

- When the clock reaches 60:00, finish any action already in progress and the referee announces “Final Round.” The current round then continues normally to completion. No new round begins.

- All remaining abilities, Reactions, buffs, debuffs, movement, and attacks continue to work normally during the Final Round.

- If neither team has been eliminated when the Final Round ends, determine the winner by: (1) most active characters; (2) highest total current HP among active characters; (3) coin flip if still tied.

## 14. Referee Authority & Safety

- The referee is the final judge of legal movement, pace distance, attack range, Line of Sight, bottle placement, cover, ability timing, Reaction timing, and ambiguous physical hits.

- The referee may reset bottles, stop unsafe actions, redirect players away from hazards, or pause the clock for real-world safety.

- Players themselves never count as cover and must not intentionally body-block balls.

- If a rule is ambiguous, make the simplest ruling that keeps the game moving and apply it consistently for the remainder of the match.

## 16. Referee Quick Reference

| Rule                 | Summary                                                                                                                                                              |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Turn**             | Two actions. Move, Basic Attack, Standard Ability, or Powerful Ability each use one action. Any two actions may be combined. |
| **Move**             | Move up to 2 paces. A character may Move twice in one turn. |
| **Powerful**         | Uses one action. It does not grant normal movement unless its card says so. |
| **Basic Melee**      | Physical throw, 1 damage, hard max 2 paces.                                                                                                                          |
| **Basic Ranged**     | Physical throw, 1 damage, hard max 6 paces.                                                                                                                          |
| **Physical throws**  | No declared target. Every legal bottle hit takes the attack’s damage/effects; any bottle contact counts. Terrain contact or leaving the battlefield ends the attack. |
| **Reaction**         | Outside turn economy. One Reaction per character per triggering attack; ally-protection Reactions may trigger even if the reactor was not hit.                       |
| **Damage**           | Add all increases → Reactions/reductions → finalize → subtract HP. Minimum 0.                                                                                        |
| **Combos**           | Different legal effects stack. No universal total-damage cap.                                                                                                        |
| **Downed**           | 0 HP immediately. Lay bottle flat; remove temporary effects on that character.                                                                                       |
| **Revival**          | 1 HP, same position, original initiative. Act later this round only if that initiative slot has not passed; spent abilities stay spent.                              |
| **Team elimination** | All 6 Downed simultaneously = immediate permanent loss.                                                                                                              |
| **60:00**            | Finish current action; announce Final Round; finish current round; then tiebreak if needed.                                                                          |
| **Tiebreak**         | Most active characters → highest total current HP → coin flip.                                                                                                       |

*Story and faction lore intentionally omitted from this rules-only edition.*
